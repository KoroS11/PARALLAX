# Provenance and Authorship Audit (K1)

## 1. Audit Methodology & Zero-Touch Invariant

This audit establishes the definitive provenance, authoring mechanisms, and source distribution across all dataset partitions in PARALLAX.
In strict accordance with the closeout safety protocol:
- **Zero-Touch Invariant**: The text column of `data/human_submissions/held_out_authors.csv` was **NOT** opened or viewed.
- **Evidence Source**: Metadata only—commit history (`git log`), corpus generation scripts (`scripts/corpus/build_corpus.py`, `scripts/corpus/generate_human_batches.py`, `scripts/corpus/benign_catalog.py`, `scripts/corpus/tool_attacks.py`, `scripts/corpus/novel_attacks.py`), file timestamps, row counts, and schema columns.

## 2. Provenance of `data/human_submissions/*.csv`

### 2.1 Generating Script and Mechanism
The files under `data/human_submissions/` (`dev_authors.csv`, `val_authors.csv`, `held_out_authors.csv`) were **not** authored by human subjects. They were programmatically generated by the script `scripts/corpus/generate_human_batches.py` (committed in `ea9793a` on 2026-10-19 17:36:46 IST) and measured via `scripts/corpus/ingest_human.py`.

### 2.2 Persona Structure
The prompts simulate 9 distinct technical personas across industrial cyber-physical systems (CPS):
- **`dev` split (60 prompts)**: `alice_ops`, `bob_scada`, `grace_grid` (20 prompts each: 10 attack, 10 benign).
- **`val` split (60 prompts)**: `carol_sec`, `dave_ics`, `eve_eng` (20 prompts each: 10 attack, 10 benign).
- **`held_out_v4` split (60 prompts)**: `heidi_water`, `ivan_chem`, `quinn_rtu` (20 prompts each: 10 attack, 10 benign).

### 2.3 Required Terminology and Display Label
- **Display Label**: `agent_authored_personas (agent-generated)` across all exports, dashboards, tables, and documentation.
- **Provenance Terminology**: Any historical references to manual contributors or persona counts are superseded and strictly replaced with the standardized label `agent_authored_personas (agent-generated)`.

## 3. Provenance of `adversarial_tools`

### 3.1 Attack Prompts
- **Producing Script**: `scripts/corpus/tool_attacks.py` (`build_all_tool_attacks()`).
- **Raw Tool Sources**:
  - Garak direct probes: `data/attacks/raw/garak/garak_raw_run.json` (category `direct`, stripped of synthetic syntax).
  - PyRIT outputs: `data/attacks/raw/pyrit/pyrit_raw_run.json`.
  - Promptfoo raw outputs: `data/attacks/raw/promptfoo/promptfoo_raw_run.json`.
  - Simsonsun/JailbreakPrompts repository.

### 3.2 Benign Rows (24 Total Controls)
- **Producing Script**: Handcrafted inside `scripts/corpus/build_corpus.py` via `make_control_record()`:
  - `dev` (8 rows): `DEV_BENIGN_CONTROLS` (operational SCADA breaker and bypass instructions).
  - `val` (8 rows): `VAL_BENIGN_CONTROLS` (historical maintenance and interlock logs).
  - `held_out_v4` (8 rows): `HELD_BENIGN_CONTROLS` (chemical reactor logs, base64 telemetry frames, OPC-UA/JSON-RPC structured queries).
- **Purpose**: Provocative-benign operational controls matching tool attack length and syntax to prevent source shortcut artifacts.
