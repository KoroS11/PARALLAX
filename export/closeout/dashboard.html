Abstain Asymmetry & Latency Audit

Across all 347 held_out_v4 prompts, exactly 10 judge abstains occurred. Forensic breakdown reveals striking statistical asymmetry: all 10 abstains occurred on attacks (10/139, 7.19%), with 0 abstains on benign inputs (0/208, 0.00%). Fisher's exact test yields p = 8.71 × 10-5.

Latency Overhead & Policy B Impact:
- Non-abstain mean latency: 5,322 ms (median 4,980 ms)
- Abstain mean latency: 7,422 ms (median 6,854 ms, +39.5% overhead)
- Hypothesis: Generation nearing the 128-token cap triggers formatting non-compliance leading to abstain.
- Under Policy B, in 7 of 10 rows, an abstain prevented a fusion BLOCK from taking effect, converting it into a FLAG.

Methodological Limitations and Threats to Validity

  • Design Split Reuse: held_out_v4 was reused during architectural design review and threshold exploration; external benchmark provides the sole pristine evaluation.
  • Synthetic / Agent Personas: The attack corpus was generated via agent-authored persona pipelines, not collected from live human attacks in industrial deployment.
  • Gandalf Ceiling Effect: Lakera Gandalf prompts are direct "ignore instructions" variants; regex D4 achieves high matching, testing narrow attack syntax rather than diverse injections.
  • NotInject Public Catalog Overlap: 30 rows in NotInject (8.85%) matched historical dev.jsonl samples and were excluded prior to evaluation to preserve independence.
  • No Demonstrated Router Skill: Learned router error prediction (AUC 0.5296) does not demonstrate skill over chance; Arm R does not outperform random routing in loose recall.
  • Sample Size Uncertainty: At N=112 (Gandalf), Wilson 95% confidence intervals span 13.6 to 18.2 percentage points, reflecting wide statistical bounds.
